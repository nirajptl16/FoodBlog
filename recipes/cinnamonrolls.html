Cinnamon Rolls

30 mins

American | Vegetarian

A traditional American breakfast dish consisting of rolled dough with cinnamon and sugar, often served with a cream cheese glaze.

Serves 4-6 people

Ingredients

    Apple Cinnamon Filling:

  • 3 apples
  • 2 tbsp of butter
  • 1/2 cup of brown sugar
  • 1/4 tsp of salt
  • 1 1/2 tsp of cinnamon
  • 1 1/2 tsp of corn starch 1 1/2 tsp of water
  • Half of a lemon

    • Cinnamon Butter Filling:

  • 4 tbsp of butter
  • 1/3 cup of brown sugar
  • 1 1/2 tsp of cinnamon
  • 1 /2 tsp of vanilla extract

    • Cinnamon Roll Dough:

  • 3/4 cup of warm milk
  • 2 1/2 tsp of active yeast
  • 1 tbsp of sugar
  • 1/3 cup of melted salted butter
  • 1/4 cup of sugar
  • 1/2 tsp of salt
  • 2 1/2 cups of flour

    • Cream Cheese Icing:

  • 1/3 cup of softened butter
  • 1 1/2 cup of powdered sugar
  • 2 tsp of vanilla extract
  • 6 ounces of cream cheese

    • Apple Cinnamon Rolls:

  • 25% heavy cream

    • Date Caramel Filling:

  • 18 dates, pitted and chopped
  • 1 cup of milk
  • 1 tsp of vanilla extract
  • 1/2 tsp of salt

Instructions

    Apple Cinnamon Filling

  1. Peel and dice 3 apples
  2. In a saucepan, add 2 tbsp of butter, the diced apple, juice of half a lemon, ½ cup of brown sugar, ¼ tsp of salt, 1 ½ tsp of cinnamon
  3. Add the corn starch slurry (1 ½ tsp of corn starch and 1 ½ tsp of water)

    4. Cinnamon Butter Filling

  4. In a bowl, add 4 tbsp of butter, 1/3 cup of brown sugar, 1 ½ tsp of cinnamon and ½ tsp of vanilla extract

    5. Cinnamon Roll Dough
  5. In a bowl, add ¾ cup of warm milk, 2 ½ tsp of active yeast and 1 tbsp of sugar
  6. Let the yeast activate for at least 20 minutes
  7. In the same bowl, add 1/3 cup melted salted butter, ¼ cup sugar, ½ tsp salt and mix well
  8. In a large bowl, add 2 ½ cup of flour and add all wet ingredients
  9. Mix the mixture until it is barely combined
  10. Wait 3-5 minutes, combine the dough and knit for 7-10 minutes
  11. Let the dough rise 3-4 hours

    12. Cream Cheese Icing

  12. Combine 1/3 cup of softened butter with 1 ½ cup of powdered sugar, 2 tsp of vanilla extract and 6 ounces of cream cheese

    13. Apple Cinnamon Rolls

  13. Roll out the dough in a big rectangle
  14. With a pizza cutter, cut the dough
  15. Spread the cinnamon butter filling and the apple cinnamon filling
  16. Form the cinnamon rolls
  17. Let the rolls poof in a baking tray for an hour
  18. Drizzle 25% heavy cream on the raw cinnamon rolls
  19. Bake for 30-35 minutes at 350

    20. Date Caramel Filling

  20. Soak 18 dates overnight
  21. In a blender, add your softened dates, 1 cup of milk, 1 tsp vanilla extract and ½ tsp of salt
