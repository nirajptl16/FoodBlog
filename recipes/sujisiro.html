Suji Siro

30 mins

Indian | Vegetarian

A traditional Indian dessert made with semolina, ghee, and sugar, flavored with cardamom and garnished with nuts.

Serves 2-3 people

Ingredients

  • 4 spoons of suji (Semolina)
  • Ghee
  • Milk
  • Elaichi powder (Cardamom powder)
  • Sugar

Instructions

  1. In a pot add 4 spoons of soji and some ghee, until the consistency is wet sand.
  2. Keep mixing until almond color
  3. Move the pot off the stove and turn off the stove
  4. Add some milk until full submerged and looks like a soup
  5. Break all the lumps
  6. Put the pot back on the stove, turn on the stove on low heat
  7. Keep mixing until all the milk is almost gone
  8. Move the pot off the stove
  9. Add some sugar
  10. Put the pot back on the stove on low and mix vigorously
  11. Add elaichi powder and mix vigorously until done
