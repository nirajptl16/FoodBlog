Paw Bhaji

30 mins

Indian | Vegetarian

A popular Indian street food dish made with mashed vegetables and spices, served with buttered bread rolls.

Serves 6-8 people

Ingredients

  • 2 peeled eggplants
  • 4 potatoes
  • 1/2 cawliflower
  • 1 onion
  • 1 bell pepper (optional)
  • 1 inch Ginger
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 2-3 bay leaves
  • 3/4 spoon of turmeric powder
  • 1 spoon of hot chili powder
  • 1 spoon of kashmiri powder
  • 1 spoon of garam masala
  • 1 spoon of paw bhaji
  • 1 red onion diced
  • 1 tomato diced
  • Handful of green peas
  • Tomato sauce
  • Lime
  • Oil
  • Salt to taste
  • Butter
  • Coriander

Instructions

  1. Half peel two eggplants
  2. Peel 4 potatoes
  3. Cut half of cauliflower
  4. Add 2 cups of water in a cooker
  5. Chop the eggplants, potatoes and cauliflower in big chunks
  6. Add a handful of peas and the chopped eggplants, potatoes and cauliflower in the cooker
  7. Add two handfuls of peas in another pot with salt
  8. Turn on the stove on high and wait for 3 whistles, then turn the stove off and let it cool down
  9. Turn on the stove on high and wait for the pot of peas to boil once
  10. Finely chop one onion and one green pepper (optional)
  11. Finely grate three garlic cloves
  12. Grate 1 by 1 inch of ginger
  13. Add 4 karchi of oil in a pan and 2 to 3 bay leaves
  14. Add the garlic and ginger to the oil
  15. Wait for the garlic and ginger to cook and add the onions
  16. Pour half a jar of tomato sauce
  17. Add ¾ spoon of turmeric powder, 1 spoon of hot chili powder, 1 spoon of kashmiri powder, 1 spoon of garam masala, 1 spoon of paw bhaji masala and some salt
  18. Cover the pan with a lid with a slight opening and wait for the oil to come to the surface
  19. Slightly warm and roll a lime
  20. Gradually add the boiled vegetables and mash them in the tomato sauce
  21. Add the peas
  22. Add some butter
  23. Squeeze half a lime
  24. Add paw bhaji masala if needed
  25. Garnish with coriander, diced onions and diced tomatoes
