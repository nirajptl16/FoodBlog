Daal

30 mins

Indian | Vegetarian

A comforting and flavorful lentil dish, perfect for a hearty meal.

Serves 4 people

Ingredients

  • 1 vatki of daal
  • 1 small tomato
  • 2 inch of grated ginger
  • 1 dry red chili
  • Red chili powder
  • Turmeric powder
  • Coriander powder
  • Cumin powder
  • Kashmiri red chili powder
  • Green chili
  • Gore
  • Lemon
  • Methi Dhana
  • Rai (Mustard Seeds)
  • Hing
  • Limbra na paan
  • Oil
  • Salt to taste

Instructions

  1. In a pressure cooker add one small vatki of daal with one small tomato and water
  2. On high heat, wait for 5 whistles
  3. Wait for it to cool down
  4. Blend your daal with tomato and some water
  5. Put the daal in a pot
  6. Add red chili, turmeric powder, coriander powder, cumin powder, kashmiri chili, little bit of green chili, 2-inch fresh grated ginger and salt
  7. Add gore and lemon
  8. Add daar masala (optional) if not add sambhar masala
  9. Let the daar boil
  10. In a vagariyu, add one fari of oil, one whole dry red chili, little bit of methi dhana and rai
  11. Turn off the heat after the rai is popped
  12. Add a pinch of hing and limbra na pan
  13. Put everything in the daal
