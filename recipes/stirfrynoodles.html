Stir Fry Noodles

20 mins

Asian | Vegetarian

A quick and easy stir fry noodle dish with vegetables and a savory sauce.

Serves 2-3 people

Ingredients

  • Garlic
  • Ginger
  • Cabbage
  • Bok Choy
  • Beansprouts
  • Carrots
  • Bell peppers
  • Broccoli
  • Spring onions
  • Black sesame seeds
  • Honey
  • Hoisin sauce
  • Sriracha sauce
  • Soy sauce
  • Noodles
  • Oil
  • Salt to taste

Instructions

  1. In a large pan add some oil
  2. Add grated ginger and garlic
  3. Add onions
  4. Once the onions are golden, add carrots, cabbage and peppers
  5. Add some salt
  6. Prepare a boiling pot of water with salt
  7. Add your noodles in the boiling water
  8. Add the bok choy
  9. Add soy sauce
  10. Add beansprouts
  11. Add noodles
  12. Add sriracha
  13. 13. Add hoisin sauce
  14. 14. Add a dash of honey
  15. 15. Add black sesame seeds
