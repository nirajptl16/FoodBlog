Mug

30 mins

Indian | Vegetarian

A traditional Indian mug recipe, perfect for a quick and easy single serving.

Serves 2-3 people

Ingredients

  • 3 handful of moong beans
  • 1 cup of water
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1/2 tomato, diced
  • Ginger
  • Turmeric powder
  • Green chili
  • Rai (mustard seeds)
  • Hing
  • Lemon
  • Oil
  • Salt to taste

Instructions

  1. Put three moothi of moong beans in a pressure cooker
  2. Add 1 cup of water
  3. Wait for 5 whistles
  4. In a bowl, grate 1 clove of garlic, some ginger, turmeric, green chili and some water
  5. In a pot, add one scoop of oil with some rai and hing
  6. Wait for the rai to pop and add the spices from the bowl
  7. In a separate bowl warm some water
  8. Dice half of a tomato and add it into the pot
  9. Let the tomatoes and spices cook
  10. Once the mixture is cooked, add the moong beans
  11. Add the warm water to the moong beans
  12. Add salt and lemon for taste quickly
  13. Let it cook for 3-5 minutes on low heat depending on the water that is in the pot without covering
